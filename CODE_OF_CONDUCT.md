# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

K.netagent welcomes respectful, constructive participation. Contributors must communicate professionally, accept technical disagreement in good faith, protect other people's privacy, and avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, or publishing private information.

## Project boundaries

Security and safety concerns are part of product quality. Do not pressure maintainers to bypass approval gates, secret protection, workspace isolation, or responsible vulnerability handling for convenience.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove abusive content and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior harms the project or its community.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository owner's GitHub profile. Security vulnerabilities must follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) and must not be disclosed in a public issue.
